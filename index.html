How it works

Hold. Hire. Dig. Split.

A holder mine on Solana where your crew size is your stake.

01

Get $DIGIT

Buy $DIGIT at launch. Holding is all it takes. No staking contract, no lockups.

02

Your crew clocks in

One Diggit for every 250,000 $DIGIT you hold, up to 80, read straight from the chain.

03

Fees buy hashrate

Creator fees from $DIGIT trading rent Bitcoin hashrate. Whatever it mines lands in a public BTC vault.

04

Split by crew

The vault is shared with holders in proportion to their $DIGIT. Bigger crew, bigger slice.
